# Privacy Policy for Toffee Twist: Sweet Match 3

_Last updated: October 29, 2026_

This privacy policy explains what information the mobile game
**Toffee Twist: Sweet Match 3** ("the game", "we", "us") handles, and how.
If you have any questions, contact us at **[YOUR EMAIL]**.

## 1. Summary

- There are **no accounts**, no logins and no game servers. Your progress is
  saved **only on your device**.
- The game shows ads from **Google AdMob**. Google may collect some device
  data to show and measure ads (see section 4).
- In-app purchases are processed by **Google Play**. We never see your payment
  details.
- The game is **not directed at children under 13**.

## 2. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following on your device (using Android's local app
storage): level progress, stars and best scores, coins, lives and their
timers, boosters, daily-reward and daily-spin dates, settings (music, sound,
vibration), whether you bought "Remove Ads", and the IDs of completed coin
purchases (so they are never credited twice).

This information **never leaves your device** and is not sent to us. You can
delete it at any time with **Settings > Reset progress** in the game, or by
uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android settings.

## 3. Information we collect

We (the developer) do **not** collect, receive or store any personal
information. We do not run any servers or analytics of our own.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to show banner ads (on the
level map), full-screen ads between levels, and optional reward videos that you
choose to watch. To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the
Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

- your device's **advertising ID** and other device identifiers,
- your **IP address** (which can indicate your approximate location),
- information about your device and app interactions with ads (for example
  ad views and clicks), and diagnostic information.

This data is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's
privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy and
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland):** before any ad is requested, the game
shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) where required.
You can change your choices at any time in **Settings > Privacy & ad choices**.

**Personalized ads:** you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of
personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or
Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for teens (rating "T") and
the game is not designed to show ads to children.

## 5. In-app purchases (Google Play)

You can buy "Remove Ads" and coin packs. Payments are handled entirely by
**Google Play**; we never receive your name, card or payment details. Google
Play tells the game which product you bought and gives an order ID so the game
can deliver it. Google's privacy policy applies to purchases:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at
children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from
children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game,
contact us at [YOUR EMAIL] and we will help.

## 7. Security

Because your progress stays on your device and we run no servers, there is no
copy of your game data held by us. Google's services transfer their data over
encrypted connections (HTTPS).

## 8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the
CCPA in California) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object
to the processing of personal data. As we hold no personal data, requests about
advertising data should be made to Google via the links above, and you can
always delete the game's local data as described in section 2. You can also
contact us at [YOUR EMAIL].

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The new version will be
published at the same address with a new "Last updated" date.
