How to play

Easy to learn, sweet to master

Every level has its own board, goals and number of moves. The first 30 levels teach you everything step by step.

1

Swap

Swipe a sweet, or tap two neighbours, to swap them.

2

Match

Line up 3 or more of the same sweet to clear them. New sweets fall in from above.

3

Twist

Bigger matches create special pieces. Swap two specials together for a giant combo.

4

Win

Reach the goals before your moves run out and earn up to 3 stars. Moves you have left turn into a Sweet Finale bonus.

Special pieces

Make specials, then twist them together

Match more than three sweets and you get a special piece. Use it in a match, or swap it with another special for something even bigger.

Match 4 in a line

Striped Toffee

Clears its whole row or column in one sweep.

Match in an L or T

Sweet Box

Bursts open and blasts the 3×3 area around it – twice.

Match 5 in a line

Toffee Swirl

Swap it with any sweet to clear every sweet of that color.

Giant combos

  • Striped + Striped: clears a whole row and a whole column.
  • Striped + Sweet Box: clears three rows and three columns.
  • Sweet Box + Sweet Box: a huge 5×5 blast, twice.
  • Swirl + Striped: every sweet of that color turns striped and fires.
  • Swirl + Sweet Box: every sweet of that color becomes a Sweet Box.
  • Swirl + Swirl: clears the whole board!
Level goals

Something new in every shop

Levels mix different goals and fun board shapes, from hearts to cupcakes.

Score & collect

Reach a target score, or collect a number of one kind of sweet.

Caramel

Make matches on top of sticky caramel to clear it. Some tiles have two layers.

Wooden crates

Match next to a crate to crack it. Sturdy crates need up to three hits.

Golden nuts

Clear the sweets below a golden nut until it drops out at the bottom of the board.

Sweet extras

Little treats every day

Play at your own pace. Every level can be finished without paying anything.

Daily rewards

A 7-day calendar of coins and boosters. Come back each day for a bigger treat.

A free spin every day

Spin the wheel once a day for coins or boosters. Spins are always free and can never be bought.

Lives that refill

You have 5 lives. You only lose one when you don't finish a level, and one comes back every 30 minutes.

Handy boosters

Rolling Pin, Free Swap, Sweet Box Start and +3 Moves help when a level gets tricky.

25 sweet shops

Walk the map from shop to shop – the Cupcake Café, the Donut Diner and many more.

Hard levels

Every 10th level is a tough challenge – and the level after it is a little easier.

FAQ

Questions & answers

When can I download Toffee Twist?

Toffee Twist is coming soon to Google Play for Android phones and tablets. Check back here – the download button will appear on this page when it is live.

Is it free?

Yes. Toffee Twist is free to play. It shows ads (only between levels and on the level map, never during play) and has optional in-app purchases: "Remove Ads" and coin packs. You can finish every level without paying.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. The game plays offline and your progress is saved on your phone. Ads, reward videos and purchases need an internet connection.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 7.0 or newer, on phones and tablets.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts or logins. Just open the game and play.

Who is it for?

Toffee Twist is a casual puzzle game for teens and adults (ages 13 and up).

What happens with my data?

Your progress stays on your phone. The ads are provided by Google AdMob and purchases are handled by Google Play. Read the full privacy policy.
