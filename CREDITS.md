# Credits

**Toffee Twist: Sweet Match 3** is an original game. It does not use any
artwork, sounds, characters, names or level designs from other games.

## Artwork
All sweets, special pieces, blockers, the logo, the app icon and the store
graphics are drawn in code with shapes and gradients
(`lib/game/render/sweet_art.dart`, `tool/branding/`). No image files were
downloaded or copied.

## Sound effects and music
All 22 sound effects and the background music loop were synthesised from
scratch by `tool/gen_audio.py` (sine waves, filtered noise and envelopes).
They were made for this game and are dedicated to the public domain under
**CC0 1.0 Universal** (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

| File(s) | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/audio/*.wav` (effects) | Generated by `tool/gen_audio.py` | CC0 1.0 |
| `assets/audio/music_loop.ogg` | Generated by `tool/gen_audio.py` | CC0 1.0 |
